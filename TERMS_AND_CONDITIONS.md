## D. Service Description — What Advance Is (and Is Not)

**1. Nature of the Service**

Advance provides a **wage access service** — not a loan. We allow eligible members to access a portion of wages they have already earned during the current pay period, based on their verified income and deposit history. Advance is not a bank, lender, or credit product. No interest is charged on amounts advanced. We do not use the word "loan" to describe this service because it is not one.

**2. No Interest**

Advances carry **zero interest**. You repay only the exact amount advanced to you. The only fees associated with the Service are the monthly membership fee and, if you choose it, the optional instant delivery fee. There are no tips, no donations, no "support us" optional payments, and no hidden charges of any kind.

**3. No Credit Check — Ever**

Advance does not perform hard or soft credit inquiries with any credit bureau. We do not use your credit report or credit score in any eligibility determination. We do not report your advance activity, account history, or repayment status to any consumer credit bureau.

**4. Advance Amount**

The advance amount is determined based on income consistency analysis using your connected bank account transaction history. Advances are limited to a conservative fraction of wages you have demonstrably earned in the current pay period based on your historical deposit pattern. We do not advance against expected future income. Current advances are up to **$25**.

**5. One Advance at a Time**

You may not hold more than one outstanding advance at a time. A new advance may only be accessed after your current advance is fully settled. You may not roll over or refinance an unpaid advance.

**6. Delivery Options**

Once approved, you choose a delivery method:

- **Standard delivery** (1–3 business days): **Free.** This is the default option. Funds are sent via bank transfer or your chosen payout method within 1–3 business days. This option is always available and never requires an additional fee.
- **Instant delivery** (within minutes): **$1.00 convenience fee.** Funds are sent to your PayPal, CashApp, Zelle, or connected bank account within minutes. This fee is for the speed of delivery, not for the use of the advanced funds.

The free standard option is a genuine, fully functional delivery method. We do not artificially delay standard delivery or otherwise make it less usable to pressure you into choosing instant delivery.

## G. Advance Settlement and Repayment

**1. Repayment by ACH**

Repayment of your advance is made by **ACH (Automated Clearing House) debit** from your connected bank account. By accepting an advance, you authorize Advance and Stripe to initiate an ACH debit in the exact amount of your outstanding advance balance. Full ACH authorization terms are set out in Section H.

**2. Settlement Trigger**

We collect settlement **based on your pay deposit arriving**, not on a fixed calendar date. We monitor your connected bank account for an incoming deposit consistent with your regular pay pattern. Once a qualifying deposit is detected — confirming that your wages have landed — we initiate the ACH debit from your account up to the advance amount.

This approach means we are settling against wages you have already received, not collecting a debt on a predetermined due date. The repayment date shown in your account is our estimate of when your deposit will arrive based on your stated payday; actual ACH settlement is triggered by confirmed deposit receipt.

**3. Manual Repayment Window**

When we detect your pay deposit, we will notify you via in-app notification and/or email. You will have a **24–48 hour window** to repay manually through the app before the ACH debit is initiated. **Manual repayment is the preferred and default-presented option.** ACH debit is a backup, not the primary collection mechanism.

**4. Overdraft Protection**

We will not initiate an ACH settlement debit if doing so would cause your account balance to go negative, based on available balance data from Stripe Financial Connections at the time of settlement. If balance data is unavailable, we may delay the settlement attempt and notify you rather than proceeding blindly.

If an overdraft or NSF fee is incurred as a direct result of our ACH debit, contact us at **advances@getbits.app** and we will reimburse the fee.

**5. Settlement Amount**

We will never debit more than the exact amount of your outstanding advance via ACH. We do not charge additional fees at settlement.

**6. Early Settlement**

You may settle your advance early at any time through your account dashboard. There is no early settlement fee or penalty. Early settlement may be made by ACH or by card with your active consent.

## H. ACH Authorization

**1. Authorization**

By connecting your bank account and accepting a cash advance, you authorize Advance and Stripe to initiate a single ACH debit from your connected bank account in the amount of your outstanding advance balance, triggered by detection of your wage deposit as described in Section G.

**2. Right to Revoke**

You have the right to revoke this ACH authorization at any time by contacting us at **advances@getbits.app** before the debit is initiated. If you revoke authorization, we will not initiate the debit. Revocation does not eliminate your obligation to settle the advance by other means; however, we will not pursue involuntary collection. See Section I (Non-Recourse Policy).

To revoke, contact us in writing at **advances@getbits.app** with your name, account email, and a clear statement that you are revoking ACH authorization. We will confirm revocation within 1 business day.

**3. Retry Limit**

If an ACH debit fails, we may attempt one additional debit retry. After two failed attempts, we will stop all automated debit attempts. We will not initiate further ACH debits without your fresh, affirmative consent.

**4. Card Fallback — Fresh Consent Required**

If ACH settlement fails, we will not automatically charge any card on file. Instead, we will notify you and present a clear, in-app option to settle by card. **Settlement by card requires your active tap/click consent at the time of the attempted charge.** We will not auto-pull a card without your affirmative action.

**5. Stripe Financial Connections**

Bank account connection is facilitated by Stripe Financial Connections. By connecting your account, you also authorize Stripe to retrieve your account balance and transaction data for the purposes of (a) deposit detection, (b) overdraft protection checks, and (c) income consistency verification. This access is governed by Stripe's Financial Connections Terms and Privacy Policy.
